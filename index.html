About Me

I am Mukesh Kumar Kachhot, a dedicated Jewellery CAD Designer with over 22 years of professional experience in transforming creative concepts into precise, manufacturable 3D jewellery designs. My work spans intricate diamond jewellery, bespoke bridal collections, and everyday fine jewellery pieces for clients and manufacturers around the world.

Over the years, I have mastered industry-leading CAD software to deliver designs that balance artistic vision with technical accuracy — ensuring every piece is production-ready, cost-efficient, and true to the designer's intent.
